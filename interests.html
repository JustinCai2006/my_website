My Interests

Outside of school and work, I enjoy trying new things and finding hobbies that keep me busy. Since moving from Queens, New York, to Boston for college, I have enjoyed exploring the city with my friends. Whether it is visiting a new neighborhood, finding somewhere to eat, or just spending time together, I like making the most of my time here. Living in Boston has given me the chance to experience a different city and meet people from different backgrounds.

One hobby that I have recently gotten into is rock climbing. I like it because it is both physically and mentally challenging. Each climbing route feels like a small puzzle, and I have to figure out where to place my hands and feet to reach the top. It can be frustrating when I cannot finish a route, but that also makes it more satisfying when I finally complete one. I hope to keep improving and trying more difficult climbs as I practice.

I am also interested in marketing and data analytics, which is why I chose to study both at Northeastern. I enjoy learning how companies understand their customers, create advertisements, and use data to make decisions. During my marketing co-op at Ropes & Gray, I got to see what working in a professional marketing environment was like. That experience made me more interested in exploring different areas of marketing and figuring out what I might want to do after graduation.

Another thing I enjoy is learning new skills through my classes and personal projects. Even though some projects can be challenging, it feels rewarding to see the final result. Building this website is one example of something new I am learning. Overall, I want to keep exploring my interests, improving my skills, and making new memories during the rest of my time at Northeastern.

Some of My Interests

  • Rock climbing
  • Exploring Boston and trying new food
  • Spending time with friends
  • Marketing and data analytics
  • Learning new skills
