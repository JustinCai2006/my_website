About Me

Hi! My name is Justin Cai, and I am a third-year student at Northeastern University. I am currently pursuing a combined major in Data Science and Marketing Analytics, and I have really enjoyed my experience so far. I am originally from Queens, New York, and I decided to attend Northeastern University after following in my cousin's footsteps.

During my time at Northeastern, I have had the opportunity to gain valuable experience both inside and outside the classroom. I completed my first co-op at Ropes & Gray, a law firm where I worked as a Marketing Co-op. It was a great experience that allowed me to learn more about the marketing industry and develop new professional skills. I also had the chance to work with amazing people who helped me grow both personally and professionally.

Outside of academics and work, I enjoy spending time with friends, exploring Boston, and trying new things. I am always looking for opportunities to learn new skills and challenge myself. Recently, I have gotten into rock climbing, which has become one of my favorite hobbies. As I continue my journey at Northeastern, I hope to gain more experience in marketing and data analytics while discovering what I want to pursue in the future.
